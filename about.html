Zacky Hilmi Imadudin Atthoriq — a UI/UX designer and graphic designer based in Jakarta, Indonesia. I've spent the last 4+ years working on SaaS platforms, enterprise operational systems, and government digital products at PT. Datasintesa Teknologi Nusantara.

My work sits at the intersection of complexity and clarity. I take tangled workflows, dense data, and multi-role systems — and turn them into interfaces that feel obvious. From the first requirement meeting to the final Figma handoff, I own the full design process.

Outside my day job, I run Srupa Labs — a small creative business focused on frames and visual decor. It keeps me grounded in product, operations, and the real experience of a customer.

I studied Visual Communication Design at Politeknik Negeri Media Kreatif (D3, GPA 3.52) and continued at Universitas Indraprasta PGRI. I'm a self-taught coder, a Figma power user, and someone who genuinely enjoys reading design system documentation.

How I work
01
Research before pixels

I don't open Figma until I understand the problem. Requirements, user flows, stakeholder context — all of it comes first.

02
Systems thinking

I design components, not just screens. Every UI decision considers the broader system, edge cases, and how it scales across roles.

03
Collaboration first

I work closely with developers and product managers. Good design only lands if the handoff is clean and the team is aligned.

04
Data-informed design

For dashboards and enterprise systems, data hierarchy and information architecture are the core of every layout decision I make.

05
Iteration mindset

I ship, get feedback, and improve. No design is ever truly finished — only better versions waiting to happen.

06
Business-aware

Running Srupa Labs taught me that design serves business goals. I keep ROI and user impact in the same frame.
