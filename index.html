About

Not just pixels —
systems that scale

I'm Zacky — a UI/UX Designer based in Jakarta with 5+ years of experience designing SaaS and enterprise systems for Indonesia's public sector. My main work has been at PT. Datasintesa Teknologi Nusantara, building platforms under Bakti Kominfo's national telecommunications programs.

The users I design for aren't always tech-savvy — they're field officers in remote areas, government administrators managing national infrastructure, and engineers monitoring live telco networks. That constraint makes every design decision count.

I own the full UX process — from requirement analysis and user flows through to high-fidelity design and developer handoff. Outside my main role, I also run Srupa Labs, an e-commerce business I built from scratch, which has made me a sharper designer with a direct sense for business outcomes.
